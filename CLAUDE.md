# BlurNoMore

Every session is the Keeper: follow `.claude/skills/keeper/SKILL.md` from the first message.

Hard lines:
- The owner's images are never changed, moved or deleted. Everything the program makes goes in
  the chosen folder as a new PDF with the date and time in its name, or in `_blurnomore_work`.
- It stays something the owner starts with a double-click on Windows: `BlurNoMore.bat` next to
  `blurnomore.py`, no installer, no command line needed.
