# Vision

DRAFT, not settled with the owner yet. Drafted by the Keeper on 2026-11-16 from the program and
its README; the owner's answers replace this.

**What it is.** A small Windows program that turns a folder of drawings collected from the web
and phone backups into one sharp A4 PDF, ready to print and trace through tracing paper.

**Who it is for.** The owner, learning to draw by tracing. Whether it is ever meant for other
people is open.

**How it should feel.** Double-click, pick a folder, press Start, get a PDF that prints right at
100%. No editing images by hand. The drawing comes out as the artist made it, only sharper and
with lines strong enough to see through the paper: restored, never redrawn.

**Where it is heading.** Open.
