# Vision

Settled in part with the owner on 2026-11-16; "Where it is heading" is still being refined.

**What it is.** A small Windows program that makes small, blurry pictures sharp and big. It
started as: a folder of drawings in, one sharp A4 PDF out, ready to print and trace through
tracing paper. That stays its core.

**Who it is for.** The owner, learning to draw by tracing. Others may use it if they want; the
owner doesn't build for them ("if others want, they can use it, don't care").

**How it should feel.** Double-click, pick a folder, press Start, get a result that prints right
at 100%. No editing images by hand. The picture comes out as it was made, only sharper: restored,
never redrawn. The upscaling is what the owner likes most.

**Where it is heading.** Two wishes from the owner, both in Refine:
- The upscaling on its own, also for photos, not only drawings for a tracing PDF.
- Previews ("previews are always good").
